# Privacy Policy: Eyes Buddy - Timer

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026

Eyes Buddy - Timer ("the app") is a countdown timer with a pair of googly eyes that live in your Dynamic Island and on your Lock Screen. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- **We collect no personal data.** The app has no account, no sign-in and no analytics.
- **We do not track you.** There is no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK.
- **The app works offline.** It never connects to the internet, and nothing leaves your device because of it.

## What the app stores on your device

The app saves a few settings locally, using the device's standard storage (`UserDefaults`, shared with the app's own widget extension through an App Group). None of it is sent anywhere, and none of it identifies you.

| Stored | Why |
|---|---|
| Timer length, and when the running timer started | To keep the countdown correct, including after you leave the app |
| Your seven quick-timer times and your most recent timer | For the quick timers and the home-screen long-press shortcuts |
| Alarm sound choice and the sound on/off setting | To play the alarm you picked |
| Dynamic Island on/off setting and the language you chose | To remember your preferences |
| Whether you have finished onboarding | So the intro is shown only once |

Your chosen app icon is remembered by iOS itself. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Notifications.** The app asks permission to send a local notification (with sound) when your timer ends, so the alarm can ring while the app is closed. These notifications are created on your device. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings; the timer still works, but the alarm will only sound while the app is open.
- **Live Activities.** The Dynamic Island and Lock Screen display is a Live Activity updated by the app on your device. It does not use push notifications or any server.

The app does **not** access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or any other personal information.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party code and shares data with no one. If you use iPhone Mirroring, Apple Watch notification forwarding, or iCloud features on your devices, those are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; the app is not involved in transferring any data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads or links to external content.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change. The app's App Store privacy label will always match this policy.
